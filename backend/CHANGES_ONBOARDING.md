# Overall New-Account Diagnostic Update

- New accounts now take one mixed 12-question baseline across all six subjects.
- Exactly 2 questions per subject and exactly 4 Easy / 4 Medium / 4 Hard questions are required.
- Logged-in subject quizzes remain subject-specific and are unchanged.
- The mixed baseline is split into subject-specific persisted assessment records so every subject can have its own trail after onboarding.
- Choosing an aim builds all six subject roadmaps immediately.
- Quiz options are now required to contain full answer text; A/B/C/D are rendered by the frontend as labels.
